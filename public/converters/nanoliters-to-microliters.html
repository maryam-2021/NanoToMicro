The Formula: nL to µL

µL = nL ÷ 1,000

To convert nanoliters to microliters, simply divide by 1,000. This is because 1 microliter = 1,000 nanoliters (10⁻⁶ vs 10⁻⁹ liters).

🧪 Real-World Applications

🧬 Molecular Biology

PCR reactions often use nanoliter volumes for precise reagent mixing. Converting to microliters helps when preparing master mixes.

Example: 500 nL primer solution = 0.5 µL per reaction.

💊 Pharmaceutical Research

Drug discovery uses nanoliter dispensing for high-throughput screening. Converting helps in scaling up to larger volumes.

Example: 100 nL compound = 0.1 µL in assay plate.

🔬 Microfluidics

Microfluidic devices handle nanoliter volumes for lab-on-a-chip applications. Converting helps in designing experiments.

Example: 1,000 nL channel volume = 1 µL for calibration.

Frequently Asked Questions

To convert nanoliters (nL) to microliters (µL), divide by 1,000. Formula: µL = nL ÷ 1,000. For example, 500 nL ÷ 1,000 = 0.5 µL.

1 microliter (µL) contains exactly 1,000 nanoliters (nL). This is because micro = 10⁻⁶ and nano = 10⁻⁹, making the ratio 10⁻⁶/10⁻⁹ = 10³ = 1,000.

A nanoliter (nL) is one-billionth of a liter (10⁻⁹ L), while a microliter (µL) is one-millionth of a liter (10⁻⁶ L). A microliter is 1,000 times larger than a nanoliter.
