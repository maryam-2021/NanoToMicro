Exact conversion tool for precision metrology, EEG neuroscience, and physics research. 1 nV = 0.001 µV (exact SI ratio).

1,000 nV ÷ 1,000 = 1.0 µV

The Formula: nV to µV

µV = nV ÷ 1,000

To convert nanovolts to microvolts, simply divide by 1,000. This is because 1 microvolt = 1,000 nanovolts (10⁻⁶ vs 10⁻⁹ volts).

⚡ Real-World Applications

🧠 Neuroscience & EEG

Brain waves and evoked potentials produce microvolt signals, while background baseline drift occurs at nanovolt scales. Converting enables precise bio-amplifier calibration.

Example: 1,000 nV EEG potential = 1.0 µV brain signal.

🔬 Precision Metrology & Thermoelectrics

Thermocouple Seebeck effect voltages and precision resistance measurements require nanovolt accuracy. Converting helps verify calibration against microvolt reference standards.

Example: 250 nV thermocouple offset = 0.25 µV thermal EMF.

Why Convert nV to µV?

  • Metrology: Calibrating high-precision 8.5-digit digital multimeters and voltage standards.
  • Biomedical Engineering: Designing low-noise instrumentation amplifiers for EEG, ECG, and EMG.
  • Cryogenics & Quantum Physics: Measuring SQUID sensors and superconductivity transitions.

Frequently Asked Questions
